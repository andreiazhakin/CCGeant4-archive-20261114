# Simulation benchmarks and software checks

## Transport benchmarks

The CSVs in [benchmarks](benchmarks/) are compact summaries of the authors' simulations, suitable for keeping in the repository. The separate [article-data release](https://github.com/andreiazhakin/CCGeant4/releases/tag/article-data-20261109) contains the event records, run configurations, digitized experimental and published-model references, plotting inputs and analysis scripts used in the manuscript. Follow the [reproduction guide](../docs/article-data-20261109.md) to verify that archive and rebuild its figures without rerunning transport. The source publications remain the reference for the experimental measurements and published calculations.

- `weick_transport_inputs.csv` lists the transport settings, target thickness or areal density and nominal entrance/exit charge selection. The status columns distinguish reconstructed thicknesses and model-based charge assignments from cases with explicit source information.
- `weick_simulation.csv` lists computed observables, statistical errors and selected sample sizes, indexed by comparison case. For the finite targets the series comprise 48 Dirac and 41 Legacy runs, each with 14,900 generated ions. The 52-row comparison index also contains four earlier equilibrium variance-rate calculations for uranium in Al. These are identified in the equilibrium-rate columns, separately from the finite-target Dirac and Legacy runs. Empty cells denote unavailable results. Use the unit columns: in the normalized-variance cases the stored Monte Carlo quantities are absolute variances in MeV². Legacy uses the point-charge electron treatment. Three cases have an additional Geant4 baseline.
- `weick_simulated_spectra.csv` contains the four selected Bi loss-spectrum comparisons binned in the centred relative **outgoing** energy. Probabilities are normalized over the displayed interval. The counts allow renormalization within that interval. Published spectra and point predictions are omitted from this compact CSV and included with their source records in the article-data archive.
- `xe_si_dirac.csv` and `xe_si_legacy.csv` give fitted deposited-energy widths for Xe-124 at 267 MeV/u through 300 µm of Si, and at 154, 197.5 and 267.8 MeV/u through 675 µm. The four matched Dirac runs contain 10,000 events each. The reported core width is 100 σ/μ in percent; its standard error is in percentage points. Full-sample relative standard deviations are a different observable.

Weick source IDs: [W-TH00, dissertation](https://web-docs.gsi.de/~weick/thesis/), [W-PRL00, Physical Review Letters](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevLett.85.2725), [W-NIM00, Nuclear Instruments and Methods B](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0168-583X(99)01025-3), [W02, uranium straggling](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0168-583X(02)00718-8). Source figure identifiers appear in the records.

## Numerical verification

[Tests](../tests/README.md) describes the executable Xe reference comparison and the Cu/Xe/U slab test. `recorded_checks/table_checks.json` and `csv_exact_export.json` summarize earlier checks on selected CSV exports, raw Dirac arrays, ATIMA reference masses and an elastic table. `recorded_checks/installed_build.json` summarizes the checks on the reference Geant4 installation after a technical update. `packaging_check.json` records the patch comparison with clean Geant4 11.4.0.
